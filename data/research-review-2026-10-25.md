# Research coverage — 2026-10-25

The review covers first arXiv submissions from **2024-10-25 through 2026-10-25** and public conference publication/acceptance records available in that window. It adds **938 unique papers** and updates **18 existing papers**. The catalog contains **1,299 unique papers** across **1,318 category listings**. The topic scope includes controlled diffusion and flow image generation, personalization, spatial and semantic control, multimodal composition, rendered text, brain/audio conditions, and associated analysis and evaluation.

## Screening and identity checks

Title screening was followed by primary abstract and metadata checks. **665 official paper pages** were retrieved with complete author lists and abstracts. Further passes inspected controller/model names and broader generation/editing titles, including EasyControl, DreamOmni2, and 3DIS.

Version-independent arXiv IDs, DOIs, normalized titles, author lists, and published-title changes were reconciled before insertion. Verified links between renamed conference papers and preprints are recorded in the JSON. Distinct papers with similar names or shared method names remain separate when their authors or contributions differ.

The [addition records](research-review-2026-10-25.json) retain complete source author lists, publication status, category, bibliography links, and a scope note. First-submission dates come from arXiv metadata; records without an identified arXiv use the verified publication or announced conference year in the statistics.

## Access and completeness limits

- OpenReview note API returned HTTP 429 and some full-text links presented a browser-verification wall; official program metadata and available primary preprints were used.
- AAAI OJS archive requests timed out; all 8,406 matching publisher-deposited 2025–2026 records were retrieved through Crossref.
- ACM conference registry searches retain exact container matches through the first mixed-container response page. They are bounded registry searches.
- ACM MM 2026 verification uses the official technical programme generated on 2026-10-21, covering 1,753 distinct main/dataset paper IDs; entries retain accepted status.
- SIGGRAPH conference-only records were resolved through the exact publisher container: 174 records under the 2025 book DOI prefix and 195 under the 2026 prefix. The 602 date-matched ACM TOG records supplement these tracks.

Coverage is bounded by the query definitions, accessible source snapshots, and pending records above. The review supplies an inspectable search and screening record; absolute completeness across all terminology and unindexed publications remains unestablished.
